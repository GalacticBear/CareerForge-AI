# CareerForge AI — V1 Technical Specification

## 1. Project Overview

CareerForge AI is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to help students and early-career professionals understand their skills, analyze resumes, discover suitable jobs, identify skill gaps, and receive personalized career recommendations.

The system combines:

- A React-based frontend
- A Node.js/Express backend
- A Python/FastAPI AI service
- MongoDB
- NLP and machine-learning techniques
- LLM-assisted explanations and recommendations

The goal of V1 is to deliver a complete, working career intelligence workflow rather than a collection of disconnected features.
